The workshop hums beneath a single light,
and the benches gather quiet, worn, and gray.
Other hands have smoothed the grain before,
their weathered marks still guiding us today.
 
There is a rhythm here the clock forgets,
a steady give and take of plane and file.
We gather up the shavings as they fall,
and stack the finished pieces in a pile.
 
The lamp burns low, the kettle starts to sing,
and through the window night is coming on.
So sweep the floor and gather up the twine,
the bench is ours until the morning dawn.
